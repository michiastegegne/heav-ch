Privacy,

This notice explains how HEAV processes personal data in connection with this website and project enquiries.

Controller

HEAV, represented by Michias Tegegne, is responsible for processing personal data on this website.

hello@heav.ch
Switzerland

Website hosting and server logs

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. When you visit the website, GitHub may process technical information such as IP address, date and time of access, requested content, browser type and operating system in server logs. This processing is necessary to provide and secure the website.

GitHub’s privacy information is available at github.com.

Project enquiries

When you contact HEAV by email or use the project enquiry form, we process the details you provide to answer your enquiry, prepare a proposal and communicate about a possible project. This can include your name, email address, company, phone number, timeframe and project description.

The form is delivered through HEAV’s Supabase backend and sent via Resend. Please provide only information that is relevant to your enquiry. Supabase’s privacy information is available at supabase.com; Resend’s is available at resend.com.

Analytics and your choice

With your explicit consent, HEAV uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, to understand aggregate website use. This can include pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser information, and how visitors reached the site. Google Analytics is not loaded unless you accept analytics in the consent prompt.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: . Your consent decision is stored locally in your browser. Google’s privacy information is available at policies.google.com.

YouTube videos

Some project pages embed video content from YouTube through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain, youtube-nocookie.com. When a player loads, technical connection data may be processed by YouTube or Google.

YouTube’s privacy information is available at policies.google.com.

Recipients and international processing

Service providers named above may process data on HEAV’s behalf or as independent controllers. Depending on the provider and your location, data may be processed outside Switzerland. We select established providers and use their published safeguards where applicable.

Retention

HEAV keeps enquiry data only as long as necessary to handle the enquiry, maintain a business relationship, comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes. Server-log retention is determined by the hosting provider.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about personal data concerning you, request correction or deletion, object to certain processing, or request restriction of processing. To exercise these rights, contact hello@heav.ch. You may also contact the competent data-protection authority.

Changes to this notice

HEAV may update this notice when the website, services or legal requirements change. The current version is published on this page.
