About HEAV

Small crew.
Heavy ideas.

HEAV is an owner-led Swiss film production company. Founded and led by Michias Tegegne, HEAV assembles the right team for every project—focused, direct and without unnecessary layers.

01 — Point of view

Relevance before noise.

We do not believe in images for their own sake. Good films begin with a precise question: what should someone understand, feel or do after the final frame?

That intended impact brings strategy, writing, direction, camera, sound and edit together. It keeps the idea visible through every production stage.
