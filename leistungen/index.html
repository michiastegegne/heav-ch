HEAV/Services
Film production · Services

One idea.
Every screen.

From strategic focus to delivery, HEAV develops moving image that works as one system for brand, audience and channel.

01 — Brand & Image Film

Identity becomes story.

A brand or image film turns identity, culture and offering into a clear cinematic narrative—not a long list of messages, but a focused and recognisable story.

HEAV supports positioning, concept, dramaturgy, direction, production and post-production—with a consistent focus on the intended impact.

02 — Campaign Content

One core idea.
Many formats.

Campaigns need more than a hero film today. We develop the core idea modularly: a main film, cutdowns, social assets, vertical formats and channel-specific versions grow from one production system.

This builds creative consistency and uses production days efficiently—without making every platform look the same.

03 — Content Creation

Continuity with character.

For brands with ongoing content needs, HEAV develops repeatable visual formats and produces moving image in planned cycles.

The result is not generic content output, but a recognisable visual language that lasts beyond individual posts.
